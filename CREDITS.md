# Model credits

## Presence sensors

The bundled presence-sensor models are original procedural approximations, not manufacturer CAD files. Enclosure sizes are editable; Everything Presence defaults are approximate. Appearance references: [Philips Hue indoor motion sensor](https://www.philips-hue.com/en-us/products/all-products/product-page.hue_motion-sensor_indoor), [Everything Presence One assembly](https://everythingsmarthome.github.io/everything-presence-one/static/assembly.html), [Everything Presence Pro](https://shop.everythingsmart.io/products/everything-presence-pro) and [Everything Presence Lite](https://shop.everythingsmart.io/products/everything-presence-lite). No manufacturer photographs or downloaded meshes are bundled.

## Tesla Model 3

[Tesla Model 3](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tesla-model-3-123c10f376ec4f18b93c73afc382808b) by **David_Holiday**, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The source model was obtained from the [redistributed GLB in the r3f-3d-slideshow project](https://github.com/wass08/r3f-3d-slideshow/blob/main/public/models/model3_scene.glb).

Adaptations: the car was extracted from the slideshow scene, transformed, normalised and quantised for embedding in a portable floorplan scene. The body was recoloured grey and the wheels darkened; the source car mesh was retained.

The adapted model is used in the private scene and is not included in the public card bundle. Attribution also remains embedded in the portable scene's model metadata.
